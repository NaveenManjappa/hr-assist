Leave and Absence Standard (NDS-HR-LAS-002 v1.2, current, effective 2026-10-01)
5 Family-related leave procedures

## **Card 5.7 — Time off for dependants and carer's leave** 

|**Element**|**Detail**|
|---|---|
|Time off for dependants|Reasonable unpaid time for unexpected emergencies involving a dependant (statutory, day one).<br>Company pays up to 2 working days per occurrence, maximum 5 paid days per leave year. Notify<br>the line manager as soon as reasonably practicable by call.|
|Carer's leave|Up to 1 week in any 12 months (statutory, day one, unpaid) in half days, days or a week, for a<br>dependant with a long-term care need. Notice: at least 3 days for up to 1 day; at least twice the<br>length of leave for more than 1 day. No evidence required. Company pays the first 3 days in any<br>rolling 12 months.|
|Approver and escalation|Line manager; Head of Department. Carer's leave may be postponed only where operations would<br>be unduly disrupted, with an alternative date within 1 month.|

# **Worked examples 5.7 — dependants and carer's leave** 

- An employee's childminder cancels unexpectedly on Monday and they take Monday and Tuesday off to arrange care: 2 paid days (time off for dependants). Later in the same leave year they have used 5 paid days in total; a further emergency day is unpaid unless they choose annual leave. 

- An employee requests 2 days of carer's leave starting Monday 8 March 2027. Notice must be at least 4 days (twice the leave). A request made on Wednesday 3 March 2027 gives 5 days' notice and is valid. The first 3 carer's leave days in the rolling 12 months are paid, so both days are paid if no carer's leave was taken in the previous 12 months.
